# Cisco AI Infrastructure: a 10-minute explainer

**Live deck:** https://ethanokelberry824.github.io/cisco-ai-infrastructure/

A five-slide talk I built from scratch in HTML and JavaScript to explain Cisco AI Infrastructure to people who have never set up an AI cluster.

- **The problem:** why companies run AI on their own hardware (control, compliance, cost) and why an AI cluster isn't just a bigger data center.
- **The network matters:** a live simulation where one congested link drops an 8-GPU training job from 50 to 21 steps a minute.
- **How it works:** how a Cisco AI POD fits together (UCS servers with NVIDIA GPUs, Nexus networking, validated partner storage, Intersight and Nexus Dashboard).
- **How it fits:** adding AI PODs next to an existing data center instead of replacing it.
- **Why now:** survey numbers on AI readiness, with sources.

`TALK_TRACK.md` has the full talk track, likely questions and the public sources behind every claim.
